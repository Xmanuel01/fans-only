Acceptable Use Policy

This platform may not be used for illegal content, non-consensual material, minors, harassment, hate, or spam.

Prohibited content

  • Child sexual abuse material or any content involving minors.
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery or doxxing.
  • Hate speech, threats, or incitement to violence.
  • Fraud, scams, or deceptive schemes.

Enforcement

We may remove content, suspend accounts, and report to authorities. Repeated violations lead to termination.

Reporting

Report violations to safety@yourdomain.com. Include URLs, screenshots, and timestamps when possible.
