Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to provide the service: account data, auth tokens, and payments metadata.

Data we collect

  • Account: email, display name, profile details you choose to provide.
  • Security: device info, limited IP data collected server-side only for fraud/compliance when legally required.
  • Payments: provider references; card data is handled by the payment processor.

How we use data

To provide access, process payments, prevent abuse, and comply with law.

Sharing

We share with payment providers, infrastructure vendors, and as required by law. We do not sell personal data.

Retention

Data is retained only as long as needed for the stated purposes or legal requirements. Payment and audit logs may be retained up to 7 years for compliance.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion subject to legal obligations.
